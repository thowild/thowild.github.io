Geshop

Geshop helps streamline Pinterest content management and publishing workflows for one account: the Gesho Affiliate Pinterest account, @geshoAffiliate.

What Geshop does

Content Planning

Organize product recommendation content and publishing workflows for the @geshoAffiliate account.

Pin Publishing

Create and publish Pins to the account owner's own boards through the official Pinterest API, after the owner authorizes access with Pinterest OAuth.

Who it is for

Geshop is a personal, single-user tool. It is operated by Thoriq Wildianto (THOWILD / Gesho Affiliate) and is used only to manage his own Pinterest account. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access, manage, or publish to any other person's Pinterest account.

How it connects to Pinterest

Geshop connects through Pinterest's official API (Pinterest API v5) using OAuth 2.0. Access is granted by the account owner on Pinterest's own authorization page, can be revoked by the owner at any time in their Pinterest settings, and is limited to the scopes the owner approves.
